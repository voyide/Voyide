When rotating a vector by $32^\circ$, we apply the following transformation matrix:

<div class="math-scroll">
$$ 
R = \begin{bmatrix} 
\cos(32^\circ) & -\sin(32^\circ) \\ 
\sin(32^\circ) & \cos(32^\circ) 
\end{bmatrix} 
$$
</div>
